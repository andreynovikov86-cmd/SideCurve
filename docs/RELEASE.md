SideCurve 0.5.5 VST3 preview and JSFX 0.4.5, with the approved AN and regular by Andrey Novikov signature.

- New SideCurve name, consistent saturated purple, larger MIX and bold labels.
- GRID and AUDIO modes, vertical INVERT, full-range detector filter and RESET.
- Drag points freely, move the transition horizontally, or lift the head/tail pairs vertically.
- Double-click point 3 to snap only points 2 and 4 to the time grid, preserving levels and bend.
- Region highlighting appears only on hover; MIX dragging is 12% slower.
- Main and sidechain waveforms use square-root scaling so quiet audio stays visible.
